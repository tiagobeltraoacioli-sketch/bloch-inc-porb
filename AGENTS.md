The user authorized real public-chain data in Graphus on 2026-09-24: bounded public chain facts may feed its graphs and analytics, with source, anchor and coverage disclosed. This does not authorize publishing private labels, identity data, customer cases or unvalidated risk scores.

The user also authorized the private `rednblue.space` portal, organization accounts, invitations, scoped API keys, and real-data descriptive Red/Blue scores on 2026-09-24. These scores describe structural deviation and observed-record completeness without judgments about persons or wallets. Keep scores tenant-private and preserve model/source/coverage metadata. Do not present them as validated AML, credit or ownership assessments. Production credentials and owner handoff files must remain outside Git and public assets.
