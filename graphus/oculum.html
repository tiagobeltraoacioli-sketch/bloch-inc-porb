03 / SOURCE BOUNDARY

Know what is covered.

BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC, and BLCH lookups are bounded to connected sources and observation times. BLCH identifiers are script hashes. Results are not an exhaustive holder index.

Point size and glow increase on a logarithmic scale with held amount within the queried sample for one asset. They do not encode transaction activity, risk, or a cross-chain value estimate.
