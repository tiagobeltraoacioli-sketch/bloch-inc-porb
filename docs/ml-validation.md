# Learning system and accuracy gates

More data can improve a model only when observations and outcome labels are reliable, comparable, lawful to use, and representative of future activity. BI-PoRB separates the following:

1. **Factual observations:** finalized transfer graph, amount normalized per asset, timing, source-linked address attributions, exact official-list identifiers, and optional private customer network context.
2. **Rules:** transparent reason codes for velocity, fan-in/out, pass-through, cyclic flows, new counterparties, and source freshness. Rules indicate review priority, not illicit activity.
3. **Outcomes:** reviewer-confirmed case resolution with evidence and timestamp. "No report" or "no dispute" is not a negative label.
4. **Offline training:** temporal train/validation/test splits, entity/cluster leakage controls, class imbalance handling, model/version manifests, and reproducible feature transforms.
5. **Promotion:** compare with the current rules baseline on precision, recall, false-positive rate, calibration, review workload, and subgroup/network coverage. Hold out recent periods and monitor drift. A model change requires documented human approval and rollback.

No model may learn from unverified sanctions hints, community allegations, or inferred person-level crime labels as ground truth. A model score must include feature/source provenance, uncertainty, expiry, and a human review route. The supplied Rust simulation is research evidence only; its reported detection rates do not validate a live BI-PoRB model.

## Implemented research components

- `service/graph_analytics.py` computes source-linked, chain/asset-isolated wallet activity, daily volumes, concentration, inter-event gap variation, descriptive regression, and bounded time-ordered paths. It rejects heuristic senders and does not turn Bitcoin outputs into sender-to-recipient edges. The output explicitly says that paths are associations and value continuity is unproven.
- `service/ml_validation.py` validates reviewed binary labels, time and entity-disjoint holdouts, and transparent confusion counts with undefined denominators left as `null`.
- `service/ml_research.py` trains a regularized logistic baseline **only** on independently reviewed examples, using training-only normalization, a bounded optimizer, and a held-out split. Its aggregate report includes confusion counts, Brier score, precision at stated review capacity, reviewed-case prevalence, five-bin reliability counts, expected calibration error, Wilson intervals for two proportions, and train-standardized feature mean shifts. It also compares holdout Brier score with a constant reference fixed by **training prevalence** and reports aggregate Jensen-Shannon distribution shift in bins fixed from training features, including holdout values outside the training range. These are diagnostics, not an operational uplift claim or a drift threshold. Missing, nonfinite, or unexpected features fail closed; there is no silent imputation. Distinct networks and assets are never pooled into one model. The report contains no case predictions, wallet score, or serving artifact.
- `service/ml_walk_forward.py` evaluates ordered, nonoverlapping future windows. Each fold trains only on cases observed **and reviewed** before the fold starts. It rejects feature availability after the observation, incomplete future outcomes, entity overlap, and mixed assets. It reports counts of outcomes excluded from training because their review was still immature. Earlier test cases may enter a later fold's training only after review maturity. Research examples may declare a SHA-256 feature snapshot identifier and source watermark no later than `feature_as_of` and observation time. The optional strict gate requires both fields on every example; missing fields otherwise mark the evaluation as unverifiable. The input manifest now commits to all feature values, labels, declared lineage, and training settings, without publishing row values. A digest and timestamp are declarations, not independent proof that the historical snapshot existed then.
- `scripts/ml_research_benchmark.py` generates 600 entirely synthetic cases across the five initial assets and runs five baseline experiments plus ten walk-forward folds. Its private output is tagged `synthetic_research_only`. The sample verifies computation and isolation checks; it says nothing about real AML accuracy.
- `service/bloch_backfill.py` collected a private, cursor-pinned sample of 400 finalized Bloch transactions. `service/bloch_sample_analysis.py` checks its digest and reports structural completeness without identifiers. `service/structural_histogram.py` exported only binned transaction-shape counts, suppressing cells smaller than five, to the graph host. `service/aggregate_ml.py` trained a weighted, unsupervised three-group structural model on those counts. The 284/59/49 groups are transaction shapes, not AML categories, wallet scores, or evidence of model accuracy. One sampled transaction had no retained input/output structure and is accounted for separately.
- A separate 495-transaction Bloch pilot used adaptive bounded pagination, passed private outpoint integrity checks, and exported only a 15-cell histogram to the ML host. Its three unsupervised groups contain 292, 59, and 136 transactions. They are transaction shapes, not AML categories or wallet scores. No raw pilot records were copied to the ML host.

The next model stage needs lawfully sourced, outcome-reviewed cases and a coverage ledger. Compare transparent rules, tabular baselines, and temporal graph models under the same entity-disjoint future holdout. Publish reviewed non-score features, regression specifications, validation methods, error bars, and limitations to the public. Only a validated AML score goes through named-person delegation. Public coefficients and wallet features must not reconstruct the protected score exactly.

The reliability bins and expected calibration error are **descriptive**, not a probability calibration certificate. Wilson intervals cover binomial sampling uncertainty conditional on the reviewed set; they do not correct selection bias, delayed labels, dependence between cases, or dataset shift. Feature mean and distribution shifts flag possible drift but do not establish its cause or a safe operating threshold. The prevalence reference is also conditional on the reviewed sample, and a positive relative Brier result would not by itself establish AML effectiveness. Before any operational use, record source watermarks, missingness and cohort coverage, label adjudication, subgroup/network splits, an external temporal test, and a documented decision threshold under actual review capacity.
