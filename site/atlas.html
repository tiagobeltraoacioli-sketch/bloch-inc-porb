03 / SAVED CALCULATIONS

Keep evidence for later review.

Save a complete path, pattern or comparison report with its source coverage, search limits and exact parameters. Reports remain available for their stated retention period even after the source analyses leave the history.

Up to 20 reports per organization, 6 MB each and 365 days. Expired reports occupy storage until an administrator purges them. Purpose, lawful basis and source rights are your declarations. Saved calculations are immutable; corrections and review decisions belong in separate records.

04 / INVESTIGATION DOSSIERS

Bring related reports together.

Reference snapshots in the portal ↗

Link saved Atlas reports to your organization's investigations. Export their verified calculations and current review records together. A closed investigation is not an evidence approval.

Select an investigation to organize its Atlas reports.

Up to 20 report links. Each report and review keeps its own expiry. Unavailable reports leave an attachment ID and digest, and are marked missing in exports. Complete exports are limited to 12 MB; oversized dossiers are rejected without truncation. Reference snapshots have a separate export in the portal.

05 / REVIEW WORKFLOW

Give every review a next step.

Assign open reviews and corrections to your organization's reviewers. Prioritize the work, set a deadline and follow each recorded decision.

Priority is set by your team. Assignment does not approve evidence, verify independence or grant access. Deadlines must fit within record retention. A reviewer who leaves the organization or loses writing access needs to be replaced.

Revision history

Every saved version keeps its original expiry. Extending a record does not revive expired history. Deleting a record deletes all its versions. Up to 100 versions may be retained per record; administrators can purge expired versions. History before this feature's release cannot be reconstructed.
